How to use
1. Pick an EDF and press Detect (5 to 10 s per hour of data; results are cached).
2. Step through the recording (←/→ half a window, Shift+arrows a full window, +/- zoom).
3. Fix marks: press Q or P then click on the deflection (the mark snaps to the nearest detected deflection); D + click deletes the nearest mark; X + click flips a P into a QRS or back. Esc leaves edit mode.
4. When every P and QRS in the window is right, press R. Only REVIEWED stretches (green band) are used for training, so mark even windows that needed no change.
5. S saves to annotations/<edf>.corrections.json. Then run train.py.
Marks: ▼ QRS (colour = morphology, ring = added by you, hollow = low confidence), ▲ P, | T end. Grey = unanalyzable signal, orange ticks on the top edge = detector's own "please check" items.
