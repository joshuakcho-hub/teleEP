Navigate

← → half window, Shift+arrows full window, + − zoom, drag on the trace to zoom into a range. Click the tachogram (bottom) to jump; drag on it to select a range for HRV; double-click it to clear the selection.

Analyze

Detect R peaks runs the generic detector on the whole file. For pigs with atrioventricular block, run teleep analyze (multi-lead P/QRS detector) and use Import marks to load its viewer_marks/<file>.marks.json; the R and P marks can then be reviewed and corrected here.

Edit

A add, D delete (or right-click a mark), Esc stop editing, Ctrl+Z undo. Added marks snap to the largest deflection within 40 ms of the click. C caliper.

HRV

Intervals spanning dropouts or unanalyzable signal, outside the species range, or differing from their neighbours by more than the artifact threshold are excluded (grey in the tachogram). The spectrum is a Welch periodogram of the spline-resampled NN series, identical to the Python package.
